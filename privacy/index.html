Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Therly Studio ("we", "us") is a web design studio based on the Sunshine Coast, Queensland. We respect your privacy and handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

What we collect

When you contact us through our website, an enquiry form (including forms on Facebook or Instagram), email, phone or text, we may collect your name, email address, phone number, business name, your website address and any details you choose to share about your project.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and talk with you about your project
  • To prepare a free website preview or a quote you asked for
  • To deliver and support the services you engage us for

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. We will only send you marketing messages if you have asked for them or they relate to your enquiry, and every message lets you opt out.

Sharing

We only share information with trusted service providers we use to run the studio (for example email, hosting and advertising platforms such as Google and Meta), and only as needed to provide our services, or when required by law. Some of these providers may store data outside Australia.

Website enquiries and optional analytics

The contact form opens an email draft in your own email app. It does not send the enquiry automatically. Please press Send in that app, or contact us directly by email or phone.

If you choose optional analytics, we use Google Analytics and the Meta Pixel to understand visits, clicks on contact links and which of our Facebook and Instagram ads bring people to the site. Meta may use this information under its own privacy policy. Analytics stays off until you accept it. You can decline or change your choice using Analytics preferences in the footer. Your preference is stored in this browser.

We do not include your name, email address, phone number, business name or enquiry text in analytics events. We exclude URL query strings and fragments from the page addresses we send. A contact-link click indicates intent to contact us, not a completed call or a submitted enquiry.

Storage and security

We take reasonable steps to keep your information secure and only keep it for as long as we need it for the purposes above.

Access, correction and opting out

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or ask us to stop contacting you, at any time by emailing hello@therly.com.au.

Complaints

If you have a concern about how we handle your information, please email us first and we'll respond within 30 days. If you're not satisfied, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.
