01What we build

Three tools. One system that grows your business.

Most businesses don't need "more tech" — they need the right pieces working together. A website brings customers in, a CRM makes sure none of them slip away, and an app keeps them coming back. Here's what each one actually does for you.

S/01

Websites

Your shopfront that never closes.

What it is. A fast, custom-designed site that explains what you do, proves you're trustworthy and turns visitors into enquiries, bookings or sales — 24 hours a day.

Why it matters

  • Customers search on Google before they call — if they can't find you, they find a competitor.
  • A professional site builds trust in seconds; a social profile alone often doesn't.
  • Bookings and quote requests arrive while you sleep, without phone tag.

What's included

  • Custom design
  • Mobile-first
  • Fast loading
  • SEO foundations
  • Booking & contact forms
  • Google Business link-up
  • Analytics
  • Domain & hosting setup

Examples

  • Dental clinic — treatment pages and online appointment booking.
  • Beauty studio — service menu, gallery and instant booking.
  • Tradie — service areas, project photos and a quote-request form.
  • Café — menu, opening hours, events and online orders.
S/02

CRM systems

Never lose a lead again.

What it is. A CRM (Customer Relationship Manager) is one place for every customer, enquiry, quote and follow-up — instead of scattered notebooks, spreadsheets, DMs and memory.

Why it matters

  • Enquiries from your website, Instagram, WhatsApp and phone land in one pipeline.
  • Automatic reminders tell you who to call back — and remind customers to come back.
  • You see what's working: where leads come from, what converts, what's owed.

What's included

  • Lead pipeline
  • Customer history
  • Follow-up reminders
  • Quotes & invoices
  • Email / SMS automations
  • Team access
  • Reports
  • Website integration

Examples

  • Salon — client history and "time to rebook" reminders.
  • Trades — quote → job → invoice, all tracked in one board.
  • Real estate — buyer and vendor pipeline with scheduled follow-ups.
  • Clinic — patient recalls and post-treatment check-ins.
S/03

Apps

Your business in your customer's pocket.

What it is. Web and mobile apps built around how your business actually runs — customer portals, booking and loyalty apps, or internal tools that replace paper and repetitive admin.

Why it matters

  • Repeat customers are cheaper to keep than new ones are to find — an app keeps you one tap away.
  • Self-service (bookings, forms, documents) frees up hours of your week.
  • Internal apps cut errors: checklists, rosters and job photos in one place.

What's included

  • iOS, Android & web
  • Logins & accounts
  • Bookings & payments
  • Push notifications
  • Admin dashboard
  • CRM connection
  • Secure data
  • Updates & support

Examples

  • Café — order-ahead and loyalty points.
  • Gym or studio — class bookings and memberships.
  • Accountant — secure portal to upload and sign documents.
  • Construction crew — daily checklists, photos and site reports.

07Questions

Good questions we hear often.

Do I need a website if I already have Instagram?

Social media is great for attention, but you don't own it and it's hard to find on Google. A website is the home base people check before they trust you — and where they can book, buy or enquire properly.

What is a CRM, and is my business big enough for one?

If you have customers you'd like to follow up with, you're big enough. A CRM simply keeps every contact, enquiry and reminder in one place so nothing falls through the cracks — it can be as simple as you need.

Website or app — which one do I need first?

Usually the website, because it's how new customers find you. An app makes sense when you have returning customers or a process (bookings, orders, documents) worth putting in their pocket. We'll tell you honestly in the discovery call.

How much does it cost?

It depends on what you need. After a discovery call you receive a clear proposal with a fixed scope and price, so you know exactly what you're paying for before anything starts.

How long does a project take?

A focused website is much quicker than a custom app or CRM. Your proposal includes a timeline for each step, and you'll see progress throughout.

What happens after launch?

We can look after hosting, updates and improvements, and help you read the results so the system keeps getting better.
