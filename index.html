Questions

Good questions we hear often.

Is the preview really free?

Yes. We design a preview of your new website at no cost and with no obligation. If you don't love it, you walk away and owe nothing.

How much does a website cost?

Every business is different, so there's no one-size price. Once you've seen your free preview, you get a tailored quote with a fixed scope, so you know exactly what you're paying for before anything starts.

Do I need a website if I already have Instagram?

Social media is great for attention, but you don't own it and it's hard to find on Google. A website is the home base people check before they trust you — and where they can book or enquire properly.

What is a booking and client system?

A simple place where every booking, enquiry and client lives together, with reminders to follow up. If you have customers you'd like to keep, your business is big enough for one.

I already have a website. Can you help?

Yes. If your current site is slow, dated or not bringing in enquiries, we'll show you a preview of what it could look like instead.

Do you only work on the Sunshine Coast?

We're based on the Sunshine Coast and love working with local businesses, but we build for businesses anywhere in Australia — Frank Windscreens is in Cairns.
